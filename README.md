# planetaty-sim
A planetary sim of newtonian gravity made with c and raylib.

CONTROLS:  
  - WASD - move in plane relative to orientation of camera  
  - Arrow keys - pitch and yaw  
  - Q/E - roll  
  - R - reset orientation
  - Tab - move slower

UNITS:
  - G = 2 u  
  - 10 km = 1 u  
  - 10 ^ 23 kg = 1 u
